# Patient Registration and Eligibility Verification

## What is eligibility verification?

Eligibility verification is confirming, before the service is delivered, that the patient's insurance coverage is active for the date of service and determining the benefits that apply: copay, coinsurance, deductible remaining, out-of-pocket maximum, covered services, prior authorization requirements and network status. It is performed electronically with the X12 270 eligibility inquiry and 271 response, or through payer portals.

## Why registration accuracy matters

Errors in patient registration, such as a misspelled name, wrong date of birth, wrong member ID or wrong payer, cause claim rejections and eligibility denials. Registration is the first step of the revenue cycle and one of the largest sources of preventable denials.

## Coordination of benefits (COB)

Coordination of benefits determines which plan pays first (primary) and which pays second (secondary) when a patient has more than one coverage, so that total payments do not exceed the charge. Incorrect COB information leads to denials such as CARC 22 (care may be covered by another payer). Medicare Secondary Payer rules decide when Medicare pays secondary.

## Best practices

Verify eligibility at scheduling and again before the visit; scan insurance cards and photo ID; update demographics at each visit; confirm whether prior authorization or a referral is required; and estimate and collect the patient's share at the time of service.
