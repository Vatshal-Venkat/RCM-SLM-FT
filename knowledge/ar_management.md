# Accounts Receivable Management

## What is accounts receivable (AR) in healthcare?

Accounts receivable (AR) is the total amount owed to a provider for services already delivered and billed but not yet paid, by insurance payers (insurance AR) and by patients (patient AR). AR is monitored by age since the claim or service date in aging buckets: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-120 and over 120 days.

## AR follow-up

AR follow-up is the work of resolving unpaid or underpaid claims: checking claim status (X12 276/277 or payer portals), correcting and resubmitting claims, supplying requested records, appealing denials, and escalating slow-paying payers. Follow-up is organised into work queues by payer, age and dollar amount.

## How to prioritize claims for follow-up

Prioritise claims by a combination of:
- Dollar value: higher balances first, because they carry the most revenue.
- Deadline risk: claims approaching the payer's timely filing or appeal deadline.
- Age: older claims are less likely to be collected; work 60-90 day claims before they age further.
- Denial recoverability: soft denials that can be fixed quickly (missing information, coding corrections) before hard denials.
- Payer behaviour: payers with known slow payment or high denial rates.
A practical rule is to work high-dollar claims nearing a deadline first, then denied claims with fixable reasons, then aged claims with no payer response.

## What causes AR to increase?

Common causes of rising AR or rising days in AR:
- Increasing denials and slow denial rework.
- Payer delays, pended claims or requests for records.
- Charge lag and coding backlogs that delay claim submission.
- Front-end errors causing rejections that must be resubmitted.
- Payment posting backlogs, leaving paid claims open.
- Credentialing or enrollment problems causing claims to be held or denied.
- Underpayments that are not identified and appealed.
- Growth in patient responsibility (high-deductible plans) and slower patient collections.
- Staffing shortages in AR follow-up.

## Reducing days in AR

Submit claims within a few days of service, verify eligibility and authorizations before service, raise clean claim rate with scrubber edits, auto-post ERAs, work denials within days of receipt, follow up on unpaid claims at 30 days, and collect patient responsibility at the time of service.
