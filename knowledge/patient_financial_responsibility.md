# Patient Financial Responsibility and Collections

## Deductible, copay and coinsurance

- Deductible: the amount a patient must pay each plan year for covered services before the plan begins to pay (reported on remittances as PR-1).
- Copay (copayment): a fixed amount the patient pays for a service, such as a fixed fee per office visit (PR-3).
- Coinsurance: the patient's percentage share of the allowed amount after the deductible is met, for example 20% (PR-2).
- Out-of-pocket maximum: the most a patient pays in a plan year for covered in-network services; after it is reached the plan pays 100% of covered services.

## Allowed amount and patient balance

The allowed amount is the maximum the payer recognises for a service under the contract or fee schedule. In-network providers must write off the difference between billed charges and the allowed amount as a contractual adjustment (group code CO) and may bill the patient only the patient-responsibility amounts (group code PR).

## Patient collections best practices

Give price estimates before service; collect copays and known balances at check-in; send clear statements soon after the remittance is posted; offer online payment and payment plans; screen for financial assistance and charity care; and follow a consistent statement cycle before any bad debt placement.

## No Surprises Act

The No Surprises Act (effective 2022) protects patients from surprise balance bills for most emergency services and for certain non-emergency services from out-of-network providers at in-network facilities, and requires providers to give uninsured or self-pay patients a good faith estimate of expected charges.
