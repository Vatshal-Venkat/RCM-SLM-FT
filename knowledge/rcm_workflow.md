# Revenue Cycle Management Workflow

## What is Revenue Cycle Management (RCM)?

Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) is the financial process healthcare organisations use to manage patient service revenue from the first patient contact (scheduling and registration) through final payment of the balance. It covers front-end activities (scheduling, registration, eligibility verification, prior authorization), mid-cycle activities (charge capture, clinical documentation, coding) and back-end activities (claim submission, remittance processing, payment posting, denial management, accounts receivable follow-up and patient collections).

## Stages of the revenue cycle

1. Scheduling and pre-registration: collect patient demographics and insurance details before the visit.
2. Eligibility and benefits verification: confirm active coverage, benefits, copay, deductible and coordination of benefits (X12 270/271).
3. Prior authorization: obtain payer approval for services that require it.
4. Patient financial counselling and point-of-service collection: estimate patient responsibility and collect copays.
5. Charge capture: record every billable service, supply and procedure.
6. Clinical documentation and coding: assign ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes and CPT/HCPCS procedure codes supported by documentation.
7. Claim creation, scrubbing and submission (837P/837I) through a clearinghouse.
8. Payer adjudication.
9. Remittance processing: receive the ERA (835) or EOB and the payment.
10. Payment posting and reconciliation.
11. Denial management and appeals.
12. Accounts receivable follow-up on unpaid and underpaid claims.
13. Patient statements and collections.
14. Reporting and analytics on revenue cycle KPIs.

## Front-end, mid-cycle and back-end

Front-end errors (wrong demographics, inactive coverage, missing authorization) are the largest source of preventable rejections and denials, so fixing data quality at registration is usually the highest-return improvement. Mid-cycle quality (documentation and coding accuracy) drives coding denials and compliance risk. Back-end performance (posting, denial follow-up, AR management) determines how quickly and completely revenue is collected.

## Key stakeholders

Patients, providers (physicians, hospitals, clinics), front-desk and registration staff, coders, billers, payment posters, AR and denial specialists, clearinghouses, payers (commercial insurers, Medicare, Medicaid), and regulators (CMS, HHS Office for Civil Rights for HIPAA).
